# NoCanva brand system

## Position

**A proper media desk for you and your agent.**

NoCanva is not a general-purpose canvas or an AI image generator. It is the shared workspace where an agent drafts within constraints, a human reviews every revision, and approved work becomes reproducible media.

Core promise: **Your agent can make the draft. You make the call.**

## Market landscape

| Product | What it owns | NoCanva should not copy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Canva](https://www.canva.com/visual-suite/) | Breadth, accessible templates, one visual suite | Everything-for-everyone messaging, rainbow gradients, template abundance |
| [Adobe Express](https://www.adobe.com/express/) | Fast social creation and multimedia editing | Feature grids and generic “create stunning content” copy |
| [Figma](https://www.figma.com/) | A shared canvas, precision, multiplayer work, connected agent context | Product-suite sprawl and enterprise-first presentation |
| [Gamma](https://gamma.app/) | Effortless prompt-to-output creation | Treating the first generated result as the finished product |
| [Recraft](https://www.recraft.ai/) | Taste, art direction, and expressive image generation | Competing on image-model aesthetics |

The open territory is the work **after generation**: constraints, evidence, revisions, judgment, approval, and exact output. NoCanva should make those mechanics feel calm and desirable rather than administrative.

## Personality

- Warm, not whimsical.
- Exact, not sterile.
- Opinionated, not loud.
- Human, not anthropomorphic.
- Editorial, not corporate SaaS.
- Direct, not “AI magic.”

## Visual language

### Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Warm paper | `#F3EFE6` | Primary canvas and breathing room |
| Deep paper | `#E8E1D6` | Desk surfaces and secondary sections |
| Ink | `#161715` | Type, dark sections, serious contrast |
| Vermilion | `#EF4B35` | One decisive signal: active state, revision, CTA |
| Proof green | `#3C7659` | Human approval and verified status |
| Muted ink | `#66665F` | Supporting copy |
| Hairline | `#CFC7BA` | Rules, grids, and physical-sheet edges |

Do not introduce purple/teal AI gradients. Vermilion should occupy less than roughly 10% of a surface so it keeps meaning.

### Typography

- `Source Serif 4`: confident editorial headlines and human judgment.
- `Fraunces`: selective italic emphasis; never entire paragraphs.
- `DM Sans`: interface, body copy, labels, and navigation.
- System monospace: prompts, hashes, dimensions, and machine state.

The contrast between serif judgment and monospace mechanics is a core brand device.

### Graphic devices

- Physical proof sheets, revision stacks, registration marks, stamps, and hairline rules.
- Visible handoffs between `AGENT` and `HUMAN`.
- Small factual labels: revision, dimensions, format, status, hash.
- Warm paper texture and restrained shadows; surfaces should feel touchable.
- Motion should communicate a workflow transition, never decoration.

Avoid robots, sparkles, glowing brains, glassmorphism, floating feature cards, glossy 3D, generic dashboards, and fake chat bubbles.

## Voice

Prefer concrete verbs and short statements.

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| “Review revision 02.” | “Unlock magical creativity.” |
| “Your agent drafts. You approve.” | “AI-powered end-to-end content acceleration.” |
| “Exact pixels, pinned template.” | “Stunning designs in seconds.” |
| “Not yet.” | “Regenerate.” |

Product copy should sound like a thoughtful studio partner. Technical proof belongs near the claim it supports.

## Product adoption

The landing page establishes the system. Apply it to the app in this order:

1. Replace global colors with the palette tokens while preserving artwork-specific brand colors.
2. Make revision, review, and approval the strongest visual states.
3. Replace generic panels with desk surfaces: proofs, ledgers, inspectors, and handoffs.
4. Use vermilion only for active work and proof green only for verified/approved work.
5. Keep navigation and form controls quiet so the artwork remains the visual object.

The application should feel like the desk shown on the landing page—not like a second marketing website.
