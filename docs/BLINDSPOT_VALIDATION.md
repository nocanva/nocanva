# Blindspot creative-engine validation

Paste the prompt below into the Blindspot agent after connecting NoCanva. Replace the source placeholders with real Blindspot reports, repository paths, or public evidence URLs.

```text
Run a blinded production validation of NoCanva Creative Engine v2 for Blindspot.

Create five separate Instagram deliverables from the five verified stories below:

1. [source for a single, specific fact-check claim]
2. [source with a strong primary-source receipt]
3. [source suited to a practical three-step explainer]
4. [source demonstrating a real Blindspot product behavior]
5. [source suited to a 3–5 slide narrative carousel]

For every deliverable:

- Build an evidence ledger first. Use only claims supported by the supplied source.
- Read the approved Blindspot brand and available compositions in NoCanva.
- Inspect the most recent 20 Blindspot drafts. Avoid compositions used in the
  previous three posts unless the story genuinely requires one.
- Supply semantic content only. Do not send coordinates, layout JSON, font sizes,
  or a visual direction on the first attempt. Let NoCanva route the art direction.
- Make the opening claim concrete: name the event, person, date, place, source,
  contradiction, or product behavior. Never use generic evidence language.
- Use a real Blindspot screenshot for product compositions.
- Review the actual PNG returned by NoCanva. Answer all eight review-rubric
  questions and treat every content warning as a required revision.
- Make at most three review iterations. Override the routed visual direction only
  when the rendered evidence gives a specific reason.
- Do not approve or publish anything. Final approval is mine.

Optimize for feed-level variety, not novelty inside a single card. Across the five
deliverables, vary composition, information density, image role, type scale, and
visual rhythm while staying unmistakably Blindspot.

When finished, return one compact table with:

- story and source
- format and slide count
- composition
- routed visual direction
- revision count
- content/mechanical warnings
- workspace URL
- your honest first-pass verdict: postable, minor edit, or reject

Then stop and ask me to inspect the five workspace URLs.
```

## Human scorecard

Score each deliverable without comparing it to the others first:

- Would I publish this without opening a design tool?
- Is the claim understandable in two seconds?
- Is the evidence visible and legible on a phone?
- Does every slide earn its place?
- Does it look deliberately composed rather than template-filled?
- Is it recognizably Blindspot without looking like the previous post?

The beta target is at least four of five rated **postable** or **minor edit**, with no unsupported claims and no repeated visual direction across adjacent posts unless justified by the story.
