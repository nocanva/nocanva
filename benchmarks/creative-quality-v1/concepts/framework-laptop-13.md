# Concept sheet — Framework Laptop 13

## Shared production contract

- Audience: laptop buyers who value long-term ownership, repairability, and configuration.
- Objective: achieve premium hardware-ad quality while making modularity tangible.
- Source pixels: official product packshots and part imagery only.
- Generated imagery: studio environment, surface, lighting, and non-product texture only. Never redraw the laptop, ports, cards, screws, internals, or markings.
- Required formats: 4:5 primary and 1:1 adaptation.
- Required editable controls: packshot selection, crop, scale and angle, shadow, callout anchors, feature order, headline, CTA, and background.

## FW-01 — This laptop stays yours

### Argument

Ownership is expressed through a product designed to be repaired and upgraded.

### Copy hierarchy

1. **This laptop stays yours.**
2. Customizable. Upgradeable. Repairable.
3. `Framework Laptop 13`

### Composition

- A pristine real laptop packshot occupies the lower two-thirds on a premium studio surface.
- One controlled opening or modular detail is shown with official imagery rather than a fabricated exploded view.
- Headline has confident negative space and minimal supporting copy.
- Neutral industrial palette with a restrained Framework-orange detail.

### Asset plan

- Official hero packshot.
- Official open or service view.
- Official wordmark.
- Generated studio environment permitted if perspective and light match the source asset.

### Fidelity and rejection rules

- Chassis, display, keyboard, ports, and proportions must remain exact.
- Do not mix generations or configurations invisibly.
- Reject if the environment lighting conflicts with the packshot, if the laptop floats, or if modularity is only asserted in text.

### Scene primitives exposed

`text`, `productImage`, `surface`, `shadow`, `lightField`, `detailInset`, `logo`, `safeArea`

## FW-02 — Choose your ports

### Argument

Expansion Cards turn port selection into a visible act of configuration.

### Copy hierarchy

1. **Choose your ports. Change them later.**
2. Expansion Cards let you configure the connections you carry.
3. `Framework Laptop 13`

### Composition

- The laptop edge creates a strong diagonal.
- Real Expansion Cards approach or leave their real bays along aligned motion paths.
- Each card is labeled directly; no generic port icons replace the hardware.
- Graphic information design meets tactile product photography.

### Asset plan

- Official laptop side or three-quarter packshot.
- Official Expansion Card images for the exact cards shown.
- Current official documentation verifying compatibility.

### Fidelity and rejection rules

- Do not show an impossible bay, orientation, count, or card.
- Avoid implying every card ships with every configuration.
- Reject if the cards are too small to recognize or if motion effects distort their geometry.

### Scene primitives exposed

`text`, `productImage`, `partImage`, `path`, `anchor`, `label`, `shadow`, `logo`, `safeArea`

## FW-03 — One tool. Every part.

### Argument

Repairability becomes credible when the real internal system and service action are visible.

### Copy hierarchy

1. **Built to open. Built to last.**
2. Replace and upgrade real parts with documented guides.
3. One sourced service fact or guide title.

### Composition

- An official internal view fills the canvas like a technical landscape.
- A real Framework screwdriver or documented service action becomes the directional focal point.
- Three restrained callouts identify replaceable parts shown in the source image.
- Editorial technical aesthetic, not a sci-fi exploded diagram.

### Asset plan

- Official internal photograph for one current model.
- Official repair or upgrade guide for the exact view.
- Official screwdriver image if available.

### Fidelity and rejection rules

- Every labeled component must be visible and correctly named in the cited guide.
- Do not fabricate an exploded assembly or expose parts from a different generation.
- Reject if callouts obscure service detail or if the poster promises universal repair outcomes.

### Scene primitives exposed

`text`, `technicalImage`, `callout`, `anchor`, `leaderLine`, `toolImage`, `citation`, `safeArea`
