# Evidence ledger — Blindspot

## Benchmark role

Software interface, evidence-led communication, and strict provenance test.

## Approved sources

- Approved Blindspot brand record in the NoCanva workspace.
- User-supplied Blindspot product screenshots.
- The primary source attached to each individual fact-check or product story.
- `docs/BLINDSPOT_CREATIVE_ENGINE.md`
- `docs/BLINDSPOT_VALIDATION.md`

## Source warning

The public domain `blindspot.media` currently resolves to an unrelated photo and video studio. It is not an approved product source for this benchmark and must not be cited or scraped for Blindspot product claims.

## Approved facts

No standing product claim is approved merely because it appeared in an earlier QA prompt or poster. Each candidate must establish its own source ledger.

The benchmark may safely assert only workflow-visible facts demonstrated by the supplied screenshot, such as the presence of a verdict, context panel, cited report, or provenance view.

## Prohibited or unverified claims

- Audience size, usage, engagement, accuracy, or performance.
- Product capabilities not visible in the supplied screenshot or documented by the owner.
- Generic evidence labels without a named source.
- Recreated or invented Blindspot interfaces.
- Marks, logos, or UI chrome inferred from old QA captures.

## Source assets required

- Current product screenshots for each chosen workflow.
- Approved logo and brand tokens from the workspace brand record.
- Named primary sources for every fact-check story.
- Date, location, subject, contradiction, and verdict where relevant.

## Candidate directions

1. **Product proof** — a real screenshot presented as a desirable interface object rather than a browser-frame placeholder.
2. **The missing context** — source-led information design with the decisive omission as the focal idea.
3. **Receipt versus claim** — primary evidence and viral wording contrasted without inventing product behavior.
