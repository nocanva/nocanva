## Approved facts

- NoCanva is an open-source, agent-native system for creating governed, brand-ready media.
- It exposes local and authenticated remote MCP interfaces.
- Agents provide research, copy, content decisions, and visual judgment.
- NoCanva owns constrained brand systems, compositions, revisions, review, approval, deterministic rendering, and immutable exports.
- Every edit creates a revision and stale writes fail.
- Approval pins an exact reviewed revision and PNG hash.
- Final rendering promotes the exact reviewed bytes.
- The hosted product uses personal workspaces and human-only final approval.
- It supports individual posters and 3–7 slide carousels.
