## Approved facts

- Framework Laptop 13 is a 13.5-inch notebook designed to be customizable, upgradeable, and repairable.
- Expansion Cards allow users to choose ports.
- The product supports replaceable and upgradeable parts.
- Current product materials describe 2.2K and 2.8K matte display options.
- The current overview describes configurations with up to 96GB of DDR5 memory and up to 8TB of M.2 storage.
- The chassis is listed as 15.85mm thick and under 1.3kg.
- Physical privacy switches electrically cut off the webcam and microphones.
- DIY configurations support user-selected operating systems, including Linux.
